# Week 8 Recap: The Catholic Church Loses Its Faith

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 8*

## The headline

Let's get right to it: the St. Brown Catholic Church lost its faith. Drake Mungo, an 18% underdog by the site's model, beat the Church 151.74 to 109.90, which makes two straight losses for a team that started 6-0 and looked like it had a direct line upstairs. Kirk Cousins went for 28.2 in the superflex and Chuba Hubbard added 24.9, and Drake Mungo still won by 41.84 even though Drake London and DK Metcalf combined for 3.2 points, which is the football equivalent of winning a game with your best two guys in the parking lot.

Josh Jacobs (23.2) and Trey McBride (23.7 at tight end) did the rest. The Church got a monster 31.7 from Brock Bowers and 18.1 from James Cook III, and then the wheels came off: Zay Flowers scored 0.0 against an 18.4 projection, Javonte Williams managed 5.7 against 19.5, and Bryce Young gave it 10.4 at quarterback. The Church is 6-2, still a lock for the playoffs and 89% to land a first-round bye, so nobody needs to start lighting candles. It also still owns the league's single-game record, the 214.84 it posted in Week 4. That's the thing about a church: it's got a lot of history to lean on.

## The rest of the slate

**Jeantrification 168.20, Bijan al-Gaib 109.82.** This one needs a name, so let's call it the Byepocalypse. Bijan al-Gaib walked into Week 8 with four of its best nine players on bye: Christian McCaffrey, Malik Nabers, Isaiah Likely and Devaughn Vele. That's like trying to hold a dinner party after the caterer, the DJ and two of your friends canceled. Jeantrification, a 65% favorite anyway, took advantage with the sixth-highest score of the season: Tee Higgins went for 37.5, Michael Wilson added 26.8 in the flex, Puka Nacua had 25.0 and Bucky Irving 21.0. Bijan al-Gaib got 31.1 from Ja'Marr Chase and 21.5 from Jordan Love, but Bijan Robinson scored 7.6 against a 21.8 projection, and Lamar Jackson left early after one snap in the superflex, so that 0.0 isn't anybody's fault. Jordan Mason scored 2.0 in the flex while Tyler Allgeier put up 36.1 on the bench, a bad beat, since Mason was projected 2.6 higher. It still wouldn't have closed the 58.38-point gap. Jeantrification is 5-3 and Bijan al-Gaib is 6-2.

**Homie G Slicer's Team 135.02, Unsolicited Nix pics 113.16.** A 37% shot for Homie G, which won by 21.86 and effectively buried a rival. Bhayshul Tuten scored 29.9 at running back, Patrick Mahomes 25.4 and Colston Loveland 15.0, and none of it mattered less than Aaron Rodgers laying 4.9 in the superflex while Cam Ward scored 25.3 on the bench. That's a bad beat, since Rodgers was projected 3.2 higher, and the 20.4-point swing wasn't enough to flip anything. The Nix pics got 20.3 from Tucker Kraft and 18.3 from Bo Nix, but Parker Washington (3.6) and Tyler Warren (7.8 in the flex) both flopped. Both teams are 5-3, though Homie G is now a spot behind on points, and Nix pics' playoff odds took a 28-point hit.

**Hampton Inn 144.64, Captain Jah'Merica 121.08.** Hampton Inn was a 30% underdog and won by 23.56, behind Joe Burrow's 39.5, the top starter score of the week, and Josh Allen's 26.6 in the superflex. Jonah Coleman added 20.4 at running back. Captain Jah'Merica got 27.9 from D'Andre Swift and 24.8 from Jahmyr Gibbs, plus it was playing without Nico Collins, one of its best starters, on bye. Michael Penix Jr. then scored -2.9 at quarterback over a bench Justin Herbert who scored 6.8, and Matthew Stafford's 8.3 in the superflex sat on top of Emmett Johnson's 25.3 on the bench. That's how a 70% favorite loses by 23. Both teams are 3-5.

**Shough My Hock 132.56, Baby Billys Bible Bonkers 60.6.** The biggest blowout of the week at 71.96, in a game Shough My Hock was a 99% favorite to win, which is basically a scrimmage with a scoreboard. Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 23.8, Braelon Allen 23.3 in the flex, Matthew Golden 20.9 and Jaylen Warren 20.3. Jacoby Brissett scored 3.5 in the superflex while Tony Pollard had 27.7 on the bench, a bad beat, and Hunter Henry left early at tight end with 13% of the snaps, so his 7.5 isn't on anybody. The Bonkers got nothing from Cody White or Omar Cooper Jr. (0.0 each) and are 2-6, which is the kind of record that makes you start thinking about 2027.

**These Bitches Love 'Doza 101.02, Tyson chicken tenders 86.20.** The closest game of the week, by 14.82, and the first win of the year for 'Doza, which was a 48% shot and finally got to exhale. Mike Gesicki scored 19.6 at tight end, Malik Washington 15.0, and Michael Mayer 12.8 in the flex, even with Jeremiyah Love leaving early at running back (19% of the snaps, 2.3 points). Tyson chicken tenders got 28.4 from Harold Fannin Jr. and 17.0 from Jameson Williams, but Baker Mayfield scored -1.6 and Keon Coleman 1.4 while Kevin Coleman Jr. had 13.6 on the bench, a 12.2-point swing that was a bad beat and not enough to flip the game. Both teams are 1-7, so enjoy the weekend, everybody.

## Start/sit report card

- **Byepocalypse watch:** Bijan al-Gaib had four of its best nine on bye (Christian McCaffrey, Malik Nabers, Isaiah Likely, Devaughn Vele), more than any team in the league. Drake Mungo (Brock Purdy, Alvin Kamara) and Jeantrification (C.J. Stroud, George Kittle) each lost two, and Bijan al-Gaib was the only team where it really showed.
- **Costliest bad calls:** Jeantrification started Kyle Pitts Sr. (projected 9.5, scored 3.6) at tight end over Dalton Kincaid (12.4, scored 20.6), a 17.0-point cost that didn't matter in a 58.38-point win. The Baby Billys Bible Bonkers started Cody White (2.0, scored 0.0) at receiver over Kyle Williams (8.6, scored 11.3), an 11.3-point cost in a 71.96-point loss, and Captain Jah'Merica lost 9.7 starting Michael Penix Jr. (13.7, scored -2.9) over Justin Herbert (12.6, scored 6.8) at quarterback. None of them cost anybody a game.
- **Bad beats, nobody's fault:** Bijan al-Gaib's Jordan Mason (projected 10.9) in the flex over Tyler Allgeier (8.3) was the biggest swing of the week, Allgeier scoring 36.1 to Mason's 2.0 for a 34.1-point gap. Shough My Hock's Jacoby Brissett (17.8) trailed Tony Pollard (14.5, scored 27.7) by 24.2 in the superflex, and Captain Jah'Merica's Matthew Stafford (14.7) lost 17.0 to Emmett Johnson (4.3, scored 25.3). Drake London and DK Metcalf were bad beats too, and none of them flipped a result.
- **Riskiest gut call:** Homie G Slicer's Team started Blake Corum (projected 7.5) in the flex over Chig Okonkwo (9.4), a 1.8-point gap against, and Corum scored 13.3 to Okonkwo's 4.2. Bijan al-Gaib's Jordan Love (15.8) at quarterback over Geno Smith (16.5) also paid, 21.5 to 18.0.
- **Dead starts:** none. Nobody started a player projected under 2 with a bench option at 5 or more for the same slot who actually played.
- **Left early, nobody's fault:** Lamar Jackson (Bijan al-Gaib), Hunter Henry (Shough My Hock) and Jeremiyah Love ('Doza). None of them changed a result.
