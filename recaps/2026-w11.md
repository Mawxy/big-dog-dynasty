# Week 11 Recap: Drake Mungo Proves It's Not a Ghost, and Bijan al-Gaib Gets Haunted by Mike Evans

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 11*

## The headline

Drake Mungo beat Bijan al-Gaib 140.40 to 137.08, a 3.32-point squeaker that the site's model gave Drake Mungo a 38% shot at, and the league's most untouchable team just got touched. Drake Mungo's playoff odds jumped from 46% to 79% in one afternoon, while Bijan al-Gaib is still 8-3 and still a lock, but the 88% bye odds suddenly look like a thing you have to actually go win. The cherry on top: Mike Evans, whom Bijan al-Gaib sold to Drake Mungo last week for a pair of second-round picks, went for 22.9 in the other uniform.

Chuba Hubbard led the way with 26.0, Brock Purdy added 22.2 in the superflex, and Trey McBride (19.3) and Breece Hall (19.0) did their part, which covered for Kirk Cousins laying a 4.4 at quarterback and Mark Andrews adding another 4.4 in the flex. Bijan al-Gaib had its own problems, starting with Bijan Robinson and Jordan Love on bye (14.7 points of lost lineup strength by our math), and then Ja'Marr Chase showed up for 8.1 against a 21.95 projection. Devaughn Vele sat on the bench and scored 14.6 against a projection of 5.56, which means Chase's no-show was worth more than the whole margin, and nobody at Bijan al-Gaib could have seen it coming. Travis Kelce did his best Travis Kelce impression with 28.4 out of the flex, and it still wasn't enough, because sometimes the universe just wants Drake Mungo to have one nice week.

## The rest of the slate

**The Cockroach Stays Un-killed, Shough My Hock Left Hanging: Un-killable Cockroach 132.18, Shough My Hock 90.92.** The Cockroach was a 64% favorite by the model and won by 41.26, with Patrick Mahomes (24.96), George Pickens (23.8) and Cam Ward (22.22) doing the heavy lifting. Shough My Hock had Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Matthew Golden on bye, a hole worth 31.2 points of lineup strength, the biggest bye hit in the league, and Jonathan Taylor (6.7) and Jaylen Warren (4.9) couldn't fill it. The Cockroach left plenty on the table, too, because Jack Bech went for 22.8 on its bench (projected 3.17) while DeVonta Smith managed 9.1, which is the kind of problem you love to have. The Cockroach is 7-4 and now 66% to make the playoffs, and Shough My Hock is a 6-5 team that fell from 73% to 48%.

**Captain Jah'Merica Bonks the Bonkers: Captain Jah'Merica 131.24, Baby Billys Bible Bonkers 50.10.** The Bonkers scored the second-lowest total in league history, missing the all-time low of 49.76 (2022) by 0.34 points, and the quarterback slot was a big part of it: Marcus Mariota put up 0.0 and Darnell Mooney in the flex did the same. Captain Jah'Merica was hit by a proper Byepocalypse, with Matthew Stafford, Michael Penix Jr. and Rhamondre Stevenson all among its best nine and all sitting out, for 9.0 points of lost strength, and it didn't matter in the slightest. Jahmyr Gibbs (27.8), Emmett Johnson (24.3) and Justin Herbert (21.44) won by 81.14, the 25th-biggest margin in league history. Captain Jah'Merica is 6-5 and 77% to make it, and the Bonkers are 2-9 and keeping their draft position warm.

**The Church Delivers a Sunday Sermon, 'Doza Doesn't Love It: St. Brown Catholic Church 151.92, These Bitches Love 'Doza 69.48.** The Church won by 82.44, the 23rd-biggest margin the league has ever seen, and the model had it as a 99% favorite so nobody is shocked. CeeDee Lamb went for 30.2, Javonte Williams for 24.1 and Jared Goff for 20.36 while Tommy Tremble put up 3.3 at tight end, with Evan Engram sitting on the bench for 16.4. 'Doza got a monster 20.88 out of rookie Fernando Mendoza, but Michael Mayer at flex (1.9 against a 12.96 projection) and Makai Lemon (1.3) took the air out of it. The Church is 8-3 and 100% to make the playoffs, and 'Doza is 2-9 and still very much in the 1.01 conversation.

**Jeantrification Gentrifies MOORE's Neighborhood: Jeantrification 178.76, MOORE 134.66.** The 26th-highest score in league history belongs to Jeantrification, and it was a 60% favorite by the model, so call it a statement more than a surprise. Dak Prescott led all starters with 35.9 in the superflex, Ashton Jeanty put up 27.9 against a 12.22 projection, and George Kittle and Ladd McConkey each had about 20. MOORE got 34.9 from Jonah Coleman and 23.1 from DJ Moore, but Josh Allen managed only 12.16 against a projection of 23.84. Jeantrification is 8-3 and 100% to make the playoffs, and MOORE crashed from 51% to 14%, the biggest drop in the league.

**The Nix pics Leave the Tenders Fried: Unsolicited Nix pics 108.34, Tyson chicken tenders 66.48.** The Nix pics were 84% favorites and won by 41.86, led by Derrick Henry (19.1), Parker Washington (18.3) and David Montgomery (15.4), while Kyren Williams and Tucker Kraft sat out on bye (9.3 points of lost strength). The Tenders had their own bye losses in Harold Fannin Jr. and TreVeyon Henderson (12.4), which is a nice excuse for a lineup that had Ray Davis and Kyle Monangai combining for 3.7 at running back. Zavion Thomas scored 10.6 on the Tenders' bench against a projection of 3.07, and the Nix pics are 6-5 with a 16% shot at the playoffs. The Tenders are 1-10, which is a sentence we're running out of ways to write.
