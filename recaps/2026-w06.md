# Week 6 Recap: Baby Billys Bible Bonkers Shock Jeantrification at 3.8% Odds, the Biggest Upset on Record

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 6*

## The headline

Baby Billys Bible Bonkers beat Jeantrification 119.80 to 108.70 after entering the week as a 3.8% pregame underdog, the biggest upset in the league's pregame-odds record. The old longshot mark was 17.3%, set in Week 14 of last season, so this one was not close. Jeantrification will look at its own bench for a long time.

Trevor Lawrence went for 27.3 and Tyler Shough added 27.0 in the superflex, while Omar Cooper (22.3) and Carnell Tate (19.0 in the flex) did the rest. Jeantrification left 52.2 points on its bench: Tre Tucker put up 26.7 and Kyle Pitts 25.5, while MarShawn Lloyd scored 1.0 in the flex and Dak Prescott managed 11.0 in the superflex. Both are bad beats, not bad calls, because Lloyd was projected 2.9 above Tucker and Prescott 10.4 above Pitts, but either swing was bigger than the 11.10 margin. Baby Billys is 2-4, and Jeantrification fell from 86% to 74% to make the playoffs.

## The rest of the slate

**Drake Mungo 136.96, Hampton Inn 69.20.** The biggest blowout of the week at 67.76. Drake Maye led the way with 23.8, DK Metcalf added 20.3 and Stefon Diggs 16.4. Hampton Inn got 18.3 from Josh Allen and little else, and Kenny Gainwell left early at 15% of the snaps against his usual 41%. Hampton Inn is 2-4.

**Bijan al-Gaib 144.24, These Bitches Love 'Doza 88.92.** Christian McCaffrey scored 30.7, the best starter score of the week, with Malik Nabers (28.5) and Isaiah Likely (26.9) right behind. Bijan Robinson managed 7.3 against a 25.0 projection, and Juwan Johnson scored 0.0 in the superflex while Tyrone Tracy popped for 18.5 on the bench. That is a bad beat, since Tracy was projected 9.3 below Johnson, and it never mattered. 'Doza is 0-6, with Michael Mayer's 20.0 in the flex the lone bright spot.

**Homie G Slicer's Team 139.60, Captain Jah'Merica 131.98.** The closest game of the week and a mild upset, with Homie G a 47% underdog. Kenneth Walker went for 27.9, Bhayshul Tuten 24.7 and Colston Loveland 21.5 at tight end, and DeVonta Smith added 20.5. Captain Jah'Merica got 23.0 from D'Andre Swift and 22.1 from Michael Penix, but Rhamondre Stevenson scored 3.6 while J.K. Dobbins put up 13.6 on the bench. That is a bad beat, since Stevenson was projected 7.3 higher, and the 10.0-point swing was bigger than the 7.62 margin. Homie G survived its own flex mistake: Cam Skattebo scored 0.0 while Chig Okonkwo had 17.4 on the bench.

**St. Brown Catholic Church 141.44, Unsolicited Nix pics 85.38.** The Church is 6-0 and has 1,009.7 points on the year. James Cook went for 24.3, Jayden Daniels 21.0 in the superflex and Bryce Young 19.8. The Nix pics got only 4.5 from Bo Nix and 5.0 from Derrick Henry, and they sat Germie Bernard (25.7) for Jaylen Waddle (6.4) at receiver. That was a bad call worth 19.3 points, and the Nix pics lost by 56.

**The Hurt Locker 123.72, Big Bucky Hunter 71.16.** Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 25.4, Matthew Golden 22.4 and Jonathan Taylor 21.1. Terry McLaurin left early, playing 10% of the snaps against his usual 72%, so his 3.7 was nobody's fault. Big Bucky Hunter, now 1-5, got 17.0 from Ryan Flournoy and 15.9 from Keon Coleman, but Jahan Dotson scored 17.7 on the bench.

## Start/sit report card

- **Costliest bad call: Unsolicited Nix pics.** Jaylen Waddle (projected 11.3) over Germie Bernard (10.0) at receiver cost 19.3 points, Waddle 6.4 to Bernard 25.7. Homie G Slicer's Team was close behind at the flex: Cam Skattebo (10.1) over Chig Okonkwo (9.2) cost 17.4, but Homie G won anyway. Baby Billys also lost 10.6 at running back with Justice Hill (6.6) over Roschon Johnson (7.0) in a win.
- **Bad beats that decided games: Jeantrification and Captain Jah'Merica.** MarShawn Lloyd (11.9) over Tre Tucker (9.1) in the flex swung 25.7 points, and Dak Prescott (19.3) over Kyle Pitts (8.9) in the superflex swung 14.5, both bigger than the 11.10 margin. Rhamondre Stevenson (14.8) over J.K. Dobbins (7.5) cost Captain Jah'Merica 10.0 in a 7.62-point loss. None of these were mistakes.
- **Riskiest gut call: Drake Mungo.** Stefon Diggs (9.3) over A.J. Brown (13.9) at receiver went against a 4.7-point gap, and Diggs scored 16.4 to Brown's 4.7.
- **Dead starts: none.** Nobody started a player projected under 2 with a bench option at 5 or more for the same slot.
- **Left early, nobody's fault:** Kenny Gainwell (Hampton Inn) at 15% of the snaps and Terry McLaurin (The Hurt Locker) at 10%. Neither changed a result.
