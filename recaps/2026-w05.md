# Week 5 Recap: A Likely Story, Bijan al-Gaib Hangs 202 on Hampton Inn

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 5*

## The headline

Bijan al-Gaib dropped **202.68** on Hampton Inn, the second-highest score in league history and the second 200-point week in as many Sundays. The Church's 214.84 from last week still holds the record, but the old mark of 195.78 from 2023 is now third. Seven days after losing to that record, Bijan al-Gaib got to be the monster for once.

Isaiah Likely did the heavy lifting at tight end with 37.9, Malik Nabers added 29.1 and Mike Evans 26.3. Bijan Robinson was almost an afterthought at 15.8. Jameis Winston even put up 25.7 on the bench, which is the best bench score in the league this week and a very expensive seat. Hampton Inn scored 137.60, a perfectly good number in most weeks, and lost by 65.08. Bijan al-Gaib is 4-1 and sits at 99% to make the playoffs.

## The rest of the slate

**St. Brown Catholic Church 158.60, Big Bucky Hunter 100.06.** The Church moves to 5-0 without needing a record this time. Jayden Daniels (32.3 in the superflex spot), Jared Goff (27.9) and Brock Bowers (27.0) were plenty, even with CeeDee Lamb limping to 2.9. Bucky Irving went for 31.5 for Big Bucky Hunter, but the bigger story was the bench: Pat Freiermuth put up 24.0 while Kyle Monangai sat in the flex and scored zero. That was a bad call worth 24.0 points, though the game was never in doubt. TreVeyon Henderson left early at 16% of the snaps against his usual share.

**Drake Mungo 137.92, These Bitches Love 'Doza 70.08.** The biggest blowout of the week. Drake Maye went for 39.5 in the superflex, the best starter score in the league, and Trey McBride added 26.1. 'Doza is still winless at 0-5, and Tyler Huntley scored 24.0 on the bench while Sam Darnold (projected 15.6) managed 8.3. That is a bad beat, because Darnold was the right start by 4.7 points, but it did not change the result.

**Captain Jah'Merica 151.22, Jeantrification 142.66.** The closest game and the only upset, with Captain Jah'Merica a 38% underdog. Jahmyr Gibbs went for 37.7 and Matthew Stafford added 27.3 in the superflex. Puka Nacua answered with 37.7 from the flex and Christian Watson scored 32.3, but Tee Higgins was stuck at 4.2. Kyle Pitts put up 17.7 on the bench while George Kittle managed 9.9 at tight end. That is a bad beat, since Kittle was projected 5.7 higher, and the swing of 7.8 points was just short of the 8.56 margin.

**The Hurt Locker 162.24, Unsolicited Nix pics 147.62.** The Hurt Locker handed the Nix pics their first loss. Jaylen Warren exploded for 36.8, Jacoby Brissett gave them 25.3 in the superflex and Jaxon Smith-Njigba added 22.8. T.J. Hockenson left early, playing 3% of the snaps, so his 2.2 was not a lineup problem. The Nix pics were hit by two bad beats: Deebo Samuel (2.5) in the flex and Jaylen Waddle (4.8) at receiver both lost to bench players projected well below them (Jaylin Noel 15.2, Germie Bernard 14.4). Neither one flipped the 14.62 margin alone.

**Homie G Slicer's Team 107.68, Baby Billys Bible Bonkers 68.58.** Homie G Slicer's Team got its second win behind George Pickens (28.0) and Aaron Rodgers (22.0 in the superflex). Kenyon Sadiq left early with 19% of the snaps, which is why the tight end slot produced 1.3. Baby Billys got 19.7 from Trevor Lawrence and little else, with Tyler Shough scoring negative 0.16 in the superflex. Shough was projected at 14.4, so that was a bad beat and not a mistake.

## Start/sit report card

- **The costliest bad call: Big Bucky Hunter.** Kyle Monangai (projected 9.5) over Pat Freiermuth (9.7) in the flex. Monangai scored 0.0 and Freiermuth 24.0, a 24.0-point cost, but it did not decide a game they lost by 58.54. The next worst was Bijan al-Gaib at quarterback: Deshaun Watson (13.9) over Jameis Winston (13.5) cost 15.5 points, and it didn't matter in a 65-point win.
- **Bad beats that nearly decided games: Jeantrification and Unsolicited Nix pics.** George Kittle (14.8) over Kyle Pitts (9.1) cost Jeantrification 7.8 points in an 8.56-point loss, so it fell just short. The Nix pics lost two coin flips in the flex and at receiver for a combined 22.3 points in a 14.62-point loss, and neither alone would have flipped it.
- **Riskiest gut call: Baby Billys Bible Bonkers.** Justice Hill (5.9) at running back over Mike Washington (8.7) went against a 2.8-point gap, and Hill paid off with 9.8 to Washington's 2.0. Big Bucky Hunter made a similar bet at receiver with Ryan Flournoy (8.3) over Keon Coleman (10.6), and it worked 12.0 to 7.7.
- **Dead starts: none.** Nobody started a player projected under 2 while a bench option at the same slot sat at 5 or more.
- **Left early, nobody's fault:** Kenyon Sadiq (Homie G Slicer's Team) at 19% of snaps, T.J. Hockenson (The Hurt Locker) at 3%, and TreVeyon Henderson (Big Bucky Hunter) at 16%. None of them changed a result.
