# Week 7 Recap: Perfect No More, Jeantrification Topples the Catholic Church

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 7*

## The headline

St. Brown Catholic Church's unbeaten run ended at six games, as Jeantrification, a 32% pregame underdog, won 173.10 to 148.24 behind its season-high score. The Church's 148.24 was the second-highest total of the week and would have beaten every other team on the board, which is the cruelest way to find the one opponent who could top it. Jeantrification is 4-3, and its playoff odds jumped from 72% to 87%.

Tee Higgins led the way with 26.8, Dak Prescott added 23.3 in the superflex, and Bucky Irving (23.0), Puka Nacua (21.8 in the flex) and Ashton Jeanty (21.6) all topped 20. The Church got 27.7 from Zay Flowers, 24.4 from CeeDee Lamb in the flex and 24.2 from Amon-Ra St. Brown, but Bryce Young managed 5.4 at quarterback against a 17.7 projection and Jalen Coker scored 1.9. Coker was a bad beat, not a bad call: he was projected 4.8 above Rome Odunze, who put up 10.9 on the bench, and the 9.0-point swing would not have closed a 24.86-point gap anyway. The Church is 6-1 and still owns the league's single-game record, the 214.84 it posted in Week 4.

## The rest of the slate

**Bijan al-Gaib 145.94, Captain Jah'Merica 143.32.** The closest game of the week, decided by 2.62 points. Geno Smith went for 23.5 in the superflex, Christian McCaffrey added 22.0 and Malik Nabers 18.1, while Isaiah Likely managed 4.2 at tight end. Captain Jah'Merica got 23.7 from Luther Burden and 22.5 from Rhamondre Stevenson, but Nico Collins scored 2.6 while Xavier Hutchinson had 10.9 on the bench. That is a bad beat, not a bad call, since Collins was projected 14.9 higher, and the 8.3-point swing was bigger than the margin. Bijan al-Gaib is 6-1, and Captain Jah'Merica is 3-4.

**Drake Mungo 146.56, Big Bucky Hunter 75.26.** The biggest blowout of the week, by 71.30. Trey McBride went for 32.1, the best starter score in the league, Drake Maye added 27.4 and Josh Jacobs 20.1 in the flex. Big Bucky Hunter got 26.1 from Jameson Williams and almost nothing else, with Baker Mayfield at 6.7. Big Bucky Hunter is 1-6.

**The Hurt Locker 142.94, Hampton Inn 72.18.** The second-biggest blowout, by 70.76, and the lowest score of the week for Hampton Inn. Jaxon Smith-Njigba scored 31.3, Braelon Allen 19.4 in the flex and Jonathan Taylor 19.0. Joe Burrow gave Hampton Inn 28.9, but Kalif Raymond and Adonai Mitchell both scored 0.0, and Sam LaPorta managed 3.3 against a 15.8 projection. Hampton Inn is 2-5.

**Homie G Slicer's Team 112.08, These Bitches Love 'Doza 99.80.** Colston Loveland scored 23.4 at tight end, Cam Skattebo 19.1 in the flex and Blake Corum 17.1, which was enough to survive Patrick Mahomes laying 5.0. 'Doza got 19.7 from Makai Lemon and 15.1 from Tyler Huntley in the superflex. Marvin Harrison Jr. left early, playing 21% of the snaps against his usual 63%, so his 7.6 was nobody's fault. 'Doza is 0-7.

**Unsolicited Nix pics 109.00, Baby Billys Bible Bonkers 104.32.** The Bonkers were an 11% underdog and lost by 4.68, and they had two bad calls that each cost more than that. Cody White (projected 3.7) scored 1.4 at receiver while Malachi Fields (5.3) put up 12.3 on the bench, a 10.9-point swing, and Mike Washington Jr. (2.7) started over Isaiah Davis (8.4) at running back, a 5.7-point swing. Bo Nix scored 25.3 and Caleb Williams 22.1 in the superflex for the Nix pics, who were helped most by the Bonkers' decisions. Unsolicited Nix pics is 5-2, and Baby Billys is 2-5.

## Start/sit report card

- **Costliest bad calls:** The Hurt Locker started Tanner McKee (projected 13.5, scored 10.5) in the superflex over Tony Pollard (14.5), who scored 36.5. That is a 26.0-point swing, and it did not matter in a 70.76-point win. Homie G Slicer's Team lost 21.0 starting Patrick Mahomes (15.3, scored 5.0) over Malik Willis (14.4, scored 26.0) at quarterback and still won by 12.28. The only ones that cost anybody a game were Baby Billys Bible Bonkers' two: Cody White over Malachi Fields at receiver (10.9) and Mike Washington Jr. over Isaiah Davis at running back (5.7), both bigger than the 4.68 margin.
- **Bad beat that decided a game:** Captain Jah'Merica started Nico Collins (projected 17.1) over Xavier Hutchinson (2.2) at receiver, and Collins scored 2.6 to Hutchinson's 10.9. The 8.3-point swing was bigger than the 2.62-point loss, and nobody could have seen it coming.
- **Riskiest gut calls:** Big Bucky Hunter's Brandin Cooks (7.9) over Jalon Daniels (11.8) in the superflex went against a 3.9-point gap, and Cooks scored 2.3 to Daniels' -0.3. Baby Billys Bible Bonkers took a 2.0-point gap in the superflex with Caleb Douglas (7.7) over Roschon Johnson (9.8), and Douglas scored 21.0 to Johnson's 1.3. Homie G Slicer's Team went against a 1.9-point gap in the flex with Cam Skattebo (6.9) over Rico Dowdle (8.8), and Skattebo scored 19.1 to Dowdle's 14.6.
- **Dead starts:** none. Nobody started a player projected under 2 with a bench option at 5 or more for the same slot.
- **Left early, nobody's fault:** Marvin Harrison Jr. ('Doza) at 21% of the snaps against his usual 63%. It did not change the result.
