# Week 1 Recap: Unsolicited Nix pics Win the Opener Without Much Nix

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 1*

## The headline

Unsolicited Nix pics opened 2026 with **166.6** points, the best score of Week 1, and ran a favored Jeantrification out of the building by 49.46. Bo Nix contributed 3.44 of it. Caleb Williams and Derrick Henry contributed 72.56.

Williams was the top starter in the league at 37.26, and Henry was right behind him at 35.3. David Montgomery added 28.9 out of the flex and Parker Washington chipped in 19.3, which covered for a 1.2 from Jaylen Waddle against a 14.7 projection. Jeantrification came in at 56% to win, and Unsolicited Nix pics' playoff odds jumped from 52% to 73%, the biggest move in the league.

Jeantrification never found a second gear. Ashton Jeanty did his part with 32.7 and Ladd McConkey added 19.2, but Kyle Pitts put up a zero at tight end against a 12.9 projection. The bench was louder than the lineup: Christian Watson matched Jeanty at 32.7, Dalton Kincaid had 20.5 and C.J. Stroud 16.46, all unused. Final: 166.6 to 117.14.

Chuba Hubbard's 23.7 sat on the bench. The Hurt Locker got 26.2 from Jaxon Smith-Njigba and 25.1 from Jonathan Taylor, but Kyler Murray finished at -0.38 in the superflex while Jacoby Brissett scored 16.48 on the bench.

**Bijan al-Gaib 134.14, Homie G Slicer's Team 108.46.** Bijan Robinson put up 29.3 for the team that wears his name, and Lamar Jackson added 24.96 in a 25.68-point win. It could have been bigger: Isaiah Likely's 30.8 sat on the bench and Ja'Marr Chase managed 3.2. Kenneth Walker's 34.1 was the fourth-best start of the week, but Homie G Slicer's Team got zeros from both Colston Loveland and Jordan Addison.

## Start/sit report card

Judged against pregame projections, not the box score. If a bench option for the same slot was projected within 1.5 points of the starter, or higher, it was a live call: a bad call if the bench player outscored him, a gut call if the starter came through, and the bigger the projection gap he went against, the better the gut call. A starter projected at least 2.5 points higher who still got beat is a bad beat, never the manager's fault. A starter who left the game early, playing 40% or less of his usual snap share, is left out entirely. These Week 1 projections were read earlier in the week rather than at kickoff, so a late scratch can look like a bad call.

- **Costliest call: Bijan al-Gaib.** Brian Thomas (projected 10.5) took the flex over Isaiah Likely (11.6). Thomas scored 6.0 and Likely 30.8 on the bench, a 24.8-point miss that the 25.68-point win covered with almost nothing to spare.
- **Jeantrification's superflex:** Daniel Jones (projected 14.9) over Christian Watson (13.7). Jones scored 9.04, Watson 32.7. Even that 23.7 would not have closed a 49.46-point loss.
- **Best gut call: These Bitches Love 'Doza.** Michael Mayer (projected 4.8) in the flex over Malik Washington (8.4), a 3.6-point gap, and Mayer won it 12.2 to 6.3. About the only thing that went right for them all week.
- **Bad beat: Big Bucky Hunter.** Harold Fannin (projected 13.7) over Pat Freiermuth (8.8) at tight end was the right start. Fannin scored 5.1, Freiermuth 18.1 on the bench, and the game was lost by 1.38.
