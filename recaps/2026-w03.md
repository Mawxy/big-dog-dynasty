# Week 3 Recap: Bijan al-Gaib Wins by 99 and Lands Saquon

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 3*

## The headline

Bijan al-Gaib hung **181.82** on Big Bucky Hunter and won by 99.24, the widest margin of the season. Big Bucky Hunter's answer was to send Saquon Barkley to the same team that flattened them, taking back Brian Thomas and a 2027 2nd-round pick. Bijan al-Gaib is 3-0 with 97% playoff odds, and Big Bucky Hunter is 0-3 and down to 0%.

Bijan Robinson led the way with 35.3, Ja'Marr Chase added 24.8, and Christian McCaffrey (21.6) and Davante Adams (20.7) piled on. The 181.82 is the second-best score in the league this season, behind only St. Brown Catholic Church's 189.02 in Week 2, and it came with Juwan Johnson's 27.3 sitting on the bench. Bijan al-Gaib beat its own projection by 31.62 and now carries a 24% title chance, second to the Church's 43%.

Big Bucky Hunter finished with the week's lowest score at 82.58, and Harold Fannin's 27.6 was a third of it. Barkley gave them 9.0, which makes 21.0 across three weeks in Big Bucky Hunter's lineup. He now joins a Bijan al-Gaib backfield that already has Bijan Robinson and Christian McCaffrey, while Brian Thomas heads the other way.

## Start/sit report card

Calls are judged against pregame projections, not the box score. If a bench option for the same slot was projected within 1.5 points of the starter, or higher, it was a live call: a bad call if the bench player outscored him, a gut call if the starter came through, and the bigger the projection gap he went against, the better the gut call. A starter projected at least 2.5 points higher who still got beat is a bad beat, never the manager's fault. A starter who left the game early, playing 40% or less of his usual snap share, is left out entirely. These Week 3 projections were read earlier in the week rather than at kickoff, so a late scratch can look like a bad call.

- **Bad calls that got away with it:** Jeantrification started Ladd McConkey (projected 13.8) over Michael Wilson (12.3) and lost 15.3 points on the swap, 10.6 to 25.9. The Hurt Locker put Kyler Murray (17.0) in the superflex over Jacoby Brissett (15.8) and lost 15.2, 10.42 to 25.6. Both won anyway.
- **Best gut call: Bijan al-Gaib.** Davante Adams (projected 10.5) over Malik Nabers (14.5) at receiver, a 4.0-point gap, and Adams won it 20.7 to 7.6.
- **Runner-up: Baby Billys Bible Bonkers.** Chris Bell (projected 3.1) over Malachi Fields (6.6), a 3.5-point gap, paid off 10.7 to 3.9 in the upset win.
- **Bad beat, Drake Mungo:** Drake Maye (projected 17.7) over Kirk Cousins (14.4) in the superflex was the right start. Maye scored 2.76 and Cousins 21.22, an 18.46-point swing in a 4.38-point loss.
- **Bad beat, Homie G Slicer's Team:** Colston Loveland (projected 11.9) over Kenyon Sadiq (6.2) was by the book. Sadiq scored 27.0 on the bench and Loveland 9.1, and that 17.9-point gap is bigger than the 14.06 margin.
