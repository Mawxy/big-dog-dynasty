# Week 2 Recap: Hampton Inn Shows Up, the Church Shows Out

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 2*

## The headline

St. Brown Catholic Church dropped **189.02** points on Hampton Inn, the best score of the season and just 6.76 shy of the league's all-time single-week record. Hampton Inn answered with 160.0, a number that would have beaten every other team in the league this week, and still lost by 29.02.

The Church got it from everywhere. CeeDee Lamb went for 35.3 and Amon-Ra St. Brown, the man on the letterhead, added 35.2. Jared Goff threw in 27.78, Jake Ferguson 22.3 and James Cook 20.9, and Bryce Young sat on the bench with 24.08 nobody needed. St. Brown Catholic Church was projected for 149.7 and blew past it by 39.32.

Hampton Inn did nothing wrong. Projected for 110.8, it beat that by 49.2 behind Josh Allen's 39.82, the second-best start in the league, plus Denzel Boston at 20.5 and Sam LaPorta at 20.2. It was a 16% underdog going in and played like it wanted the upset. It just drew the one team that was going to be better.

The result: St. Brown Catholic Church is 2-0 with 347.7 points for, 50.8 clear of anyone else, and the sim gives it a 37% title chance, the best in the league.

**Homie G Slicer's Team 123.16, Big Bucky Hunter 65.28.** Homie G Slicer's Team's first win of the year, built on Patrick Mahomes (28.98), DeVonta Smith (27.7) and Kenneth Walker (23.8). Colston Loveland followed up his Week 1 zero with 1.8 against a 14.2 projection, so the tight end spot is still a problem. Big Bucky Hunter got 0.8 from Jaxson Dart, projected for 16.4, and 3.0 from Saquon Barkley, and the 65.28 is the second-lowest score of the season.

**Jeantrification 142.78, These Bitches Love 'Doza 56.32.** The biggest blowout of the week, margin 86.46. Dak Prescott (29.76) and Dalton Kincaid (26.0) led the way, and Jeantrification had enough to leave Tre Tucker's 22.9 and George Kittle's 20.0 on the bench. These Bitches Love 'Doza started a quarterback who scored -0.56 and a receiver who scored 0.0, and the 56.32 is the season low, 8.8 points above the league's all-time worst.

**The Hurt Locker 138.16, Captain Jah'Merica 101.56.** Jaxon Smith-Njigba went for 42.5, the best start of the week, and Jonathan Taylor added 29.2. Captain Jah'Merica got 26.98 from Matthew Stafford and 23.3 from Jahmyr Gibbs, but Justin Herbert (4.88) and Dallas Goedert (1.9) dragged it down. Dalton Schultz scored 32.0 on the bench.

## Start/sit report card

Calls are judged against pregame projections, not the box score, and these Week 2 projections were read earlier in the week rather than at kickoff, so a late scratch can look like a stale projection.

- **Worst call: Captain Jah'Merica.** Xavier Hutchinson (projected 5.6) started over Dalton Schultz (11.8). Hutchinson scored 6.7, Schultz 32.0, a 25.3-point miss. It did not cost the game, though: even with Schultz, Captain Jah'Merica comes up 11.3 short.
- **Dead starts: These Bitches Love 'Doza.** Cooper Rush and Mack Hollins were both projected for 0.0 and both started. Rush went over Darren Waller (projected 7.5, scored 19.8) and Hollins over Malik Washington (8.9, scored 8.7). That is 25.3 points left on the table, not that it would have mattered in an 86.46-point loss.
- **Gut call of the week: Unsolicited Nix pics.** Parker Washington (projected 10.9) got the start over Tucker Kraft (14.9). Washington scored 15.8, Kraft 3.5. That 12.3-point swing is bigger than the 4.64 margin, so without it the Nix pics lose.
- **Smaller gut call: Bijan al-Gaib.** Aaron Jones (projected 9.9) over Mike Evans (13.6) paid off 10.5 to 8.4.
- **Bad beat: Drake Mungo.** Drake Maye (projected 16.9) over Kirk Cousins (11.9) was the right start. Maye scored 8.02, Cousins 20.22, and the 12.2-point gap was more than the 4.64 Drake Mungo lost by. Not the manager's fault.
