# Week 2 Recap: Hampton Inn Shows Up, the Church Shows Out

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 2*

## The headline

St. Brown Catholic Church dropped **189.02** points on Hampton Inn, the best score of the season and just 6.76 shy of the league's all-time single-week record. Hampton Inn answered with 160.0, a number that would have beaten every other team in the league this week, and still lost by 29.02.

The Church got it from everywhere. CeeDee Lamb went for 35.3 and Amon-Ra St. Brown, the man on the letterhead, added 35.2. Jared Goff threw in 27.78, Jake Ferguson 22.3 and James Cook 20.9, and Bryce Young sat on the bench with 24.08 nobody needed. St. Brown Catholic Church was projected for 149.7 and blew past it by 39.32.

Hampton Inn did nothing wrong. Projected for 110.8, it beat that by 49.2 behind Josh Allen's 39.82, the second-best start in the league, plus Denzel Boston at 20.5 and Sam LaPorta at 20.2. It was a 16% underdog going in and played like it wanted the upset. It just drew the one team that was going to be better.

The result: St. Brown Catholic Church is 2-0 with 347.7 points for, 50.8 clear of anyone else, and the sim gives it a 37% title chance, the best in the league.

## Start/sit report card

Calls are judged against pregame projections, not the box score. If a bench option for the same slot was projected within 1.5 points of the starter, or higher, it was a live call: a bad call if the bench player outscored him, a gut call if the starter came through, and the bigger the projection gap he went against, the better the gut call. A starter projected at least 2.5 points higher who still got beat is a bad beat, never the manager's fault. These Week 2 projections were read earlier in the week rather than at kickoff, so a late scratch can look like a bad call.

- **Worst call: Captain Jah'Merica.** Dallas Goedert (projected 11.7) at tight end over Dalton Schultz (11.8). Goedert scored 1.9 and Schultz 32.0 on the bench, a 30.1-point miss. Even with Schultz, Captain Jah'Merica comes up 6.5 short.
- **The call that cost a game: Drake Mungo.** DK Metcalf and Chuba Hubbard were both projected 12.0, and Metcalf got the flex. He scored 6.7, Hubbard 14.4, and that 7.7 is more than the 4.64 they lost by. The superflex was a bad beat on top of it: Drake Maye (16.9) over Kirk Cousins (11.9) was the right start, and Cousins outscored him 20.22 to 8.02 from the bench.
- **Dead start: These Bitches Love 'Doza.** Cooper Rush went in the superflex on a 0.0 projection and scored -0.56, with Darren Waller (projected 7.5) on the bench for 19.8.
- **Best gut call: Bijan al-Gaib.** Davante Adams (projected 12.1) over Mike Evans (13.6) at receiver, and Adams went for 39.5 to Evans' 8.4.
- **Runner-up: Unsolicited Nix pics.** Tyler Warren (projected 13.5) over Tucker Kraft (14.9) at tight end paid off 17.4 to 3.5, more than the 4.64 they won by.
