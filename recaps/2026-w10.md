# Week 10 Recap: The Cockroach Crawls Out of the Wreckage

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 10*

## The headline

Last week we wrote the obituary for the Un-killable Cockroach, and this week it walked into Drake Mungo's house, ate the leftovers and took the car keys. The Cockroach won 174.34 to 153.18 in a game the site's model gave it a 15% shot at, and its playoff odds jumped from 28% to 67% while Drake Mungo's fell from 86% to 46%. George Pickens went for 43.5, Patrick Mahomes added 35.42, and Drake Mungo lost Drake Maye after he saw about 17% of his usual snaps (3.02 points against a 21.93 projection), which is the football equivalent of the lights going out during the big speech.

Pickens was the best receiver among all starters this week and the third-best starter overall, behind Christian McCaffrey (46.3) and Jahmyr Gibbs (44.2). Cam Skattebo gave the Cockroach 27.2 from the flex, Blake Corum had 20.9, and Bhayshul Tuten chipped in 20.3, so even with Wan'Dale Robinson laying a 4.0 on the field, the whole machine hummed. Drake Mungo still got 34.2 from Chuba Hubbard and 31.66 from Brock Purdy and dropped 153 points, which would have won most weeks, and instead it is now 6-4 and sitting on a coin flip. Drake Mungo has spent the week looking like the movie Lake Mungo, where everybody keeps staring at the photographs and wondering who that is lurking in the background of a season that was supposed to be fine, and the answer is a quarterback's injury report. The injury news is the other big story, because Maye's early exit is the one that really hurts, and it comes in a week when even the NFL couldn't stay healthy, with Aidan Hutchinson getting hurt celebrating, a reminder that the most dangerous thing in football is joy. Nobody is calling the Cockroach un-killable again, but we're also not stupid enough to say it's dead.

## The rest of the slate

**Bijan al-Gaib 185.28, Shough My Hock 125.08.** The 13th-highest score in league history belongs to Bijan al-Gaib, and the site's model had it as a 73% favorite, which looks cute now. Christian McCaffrey went for 46.3, Ja'Marr Chase for 33.1, Isaiah Likely for 31.2 out of the flex (projected 14.63, because tight ends are apparently allowed to do that now) and Trevor Lawrence for 26.78, while Travis Kelce, Malik Nabers and Chris Olave combined for 19.4 and nobody noticed. Shough My Hock got 31.1 from Jaxon Smith-Njigba and 23.9 from Tony Pollard, but Jaylen Warren came back from his bye to score 8.1 and Hunter Henry had 5.2 at tight end, so the 60.20-point loss was a team effort. Bijan al-Gaib is 8-2 and Shough is 6-4 and 64% to make the playoffs, down 16 points.

**Captain Jah'Merica 172.20, St. Brown Catholic Church 160.08.** This is the one where the Church lost a game it scored 160 points in, which should be illegal. Captain Jah'Merica had three starters on bye (D'Andre Swift, Luther Burden III and Dallas Goedert, about 16.2 points of lost lineup strength) and still got 44.2 from Gibbs and 34.1 from Emmett Johnson, because that's how you handle a Byepocalypse, by ignoring it. The deciding move was in the flex, where Xavier Hutchinson (projected 6.15) went for 15.8 and the safer Rhamondre Stevenson (12.15) scored 1.5, a 14.3-point swing in a game decided by 12.12, so a rare riskier-than-the-projection call that actually won the weekend. The Church lost Brock Bowers to an early exit (19% of his usual snaps, 4.8 against an 18.0 projection) and Chase Brown (28.3) and Amon-Ra St. Brown (25.8) couldn't fully cover. Captain Jah'Merica was a 13% underdog by the model and is now 5-5 with 64% playoff odds.

**Jeantrification 138.06, Tyson chicken tenders 75.50.** A 98% favorite did its job, winning by 62.56, the 53rd-biggest margin in league history. Dak Prescott went for 34.42 in the superflex, C.J. Stroud had 20.44 and George Kittle 16.4, and the Tenders sent Tua Tagovailoa out at quarterback for a flat 0.0 while Baker Mayfield and Kyle Monangai sat on bye for 17.0 lost points. Jordyn Tyson put up 19.0 on the Tenders bench as Keon Coleman scored 2.1 at receiver, and the Tenders are 1-9, so the highlight reel for this one is basically over. Jeantrification is 7-3 and 93% to make the playoffs, down 2 on the week.

**MOORE 106.40, Unsolicited Nix pics 85.20.** The team formerly known as Hampton Inn got a monster from Joe Burrow (33.16) and 22.34 from Josh Allen, then watched Josh Downs and Devin Singletary leave early and still won by 21.2, which says plenty about the Nix pics. Caleb Williams and Bo Nix sat on bye (20.3 points of lost lineup strength), Shedeur Sanders scored 0.0 at quarterback, and the real killer was the bench, where Deebo Samuel Sr. dropped 25.9 while Germie Bernard started at receiver and scored 1.0, a 24.9-point miss in a 21.2-point loss. MOORE is 5-5 and 47% to make the playoffs, up 12 points, and the Nix pics are 5-5 and 18%, down 22.

**These Bitches Love 'Doza 56.18, Baby Billys Bible Bonkers 53.84.** The tanking Super Bowl turned into a bad-offense film festival, with the Bonkers' 53.84 landing as the fourth-lowest score in league history and the 'Doza's 56.18 the eighth-lowest. The 'Doza won by 2.34 as a 75% favorite on 13.92 from Fernando Mendoza and 13.36 from Malik Washington, and still left 9.2 on the bench by starting Chris Rodriguez Jr. (0.5) over Lan Larison (9.7). The Bonkers could have flipped it by starting Malachi Fields (9.2) over Jaylen Wright (3.5) in the flex, and Omar Cooper Jr. and Greg Dulcich each gave them a flat 0.0. Both teams are 2-8, so the real winner here is anybody holding a pick in the 2027 draft.

## Start/sit report card

- **Costliest bad calls:** Unsolicited Nix pics started Germie Bernard (projected 11.12, scored 1.0) at receiver over Deebo Samuel Sr. (10.93, scored 25.9), a 24.9-point swing in a game they lost by 21.2, so it was the game. Tyson chicken tenders gave up 16.9 with Keon Coleman (7.57, scored 2.1) over Jordyn Tyson (7.96, scored 19.0) at receiver in a 62.6-point loss. Baby Billys Bible Bonkers lost 5.7 with Jaylen Wright (2.73, scored 3.5) over Malachi Fields (6.51, scored 9.2) in the flex, more than the 2.34 they lost by. Bijan al-Gaib started Jordan Love (16.08, scored 10.8) over Deshaun Watson (14.74, scored 20.38), a 9.6-point miss, and These Bitches Love 'Doza lost 9.2 with Chris Rodriguez Jr. (7.73, scored 0.5) over Lan Larison (8.21, scored 9.7), though neither moved a result.
- **Bad beats, nobody's fault:** Bijan al-Gaib started Chris Olave (16.23, scored 6.0) with DJ Moore (12.16) on the bench and Moore popped for 19.4, a 13.4-point swing. MOORE had Rachaad White (10.97, scored 6.2) at running back with Omarion Hampton (7.83) scoring 16.5 on the bench, and Drake Mungo had Trey McBride (17.77, scored 11.2) at tight end while Mark Andrews (8.55) went for 20.2 on the bench. The only one that touched a result was Omar Cooper Jr. (8.54, scored 0.0) for the Baby Billys Bible Bonkers, with Darnell Mooney (3.52) scoring 2.8 on the bench, in a game they lost by 2.34.
- **Riskiest gut calls:** Captain Jah'Merica went against a 6.0-point projection gap in the flex, starting Xavier Hutchinson (6.15) over Rhamondre Stevenson (12.15), and Hutchinson scored 15.8 to Stevenson's 1.5, which won them the game. Drake Mungo started Stefon Diggs (10.31) in the flex over DK Metcalf (12.49), a 2.2-point gap, and Diggs scored 20.3 to Metcalf's 15.4.
- **Dead starts:** none.
- **Left early, nobody's fault:** Drake Maye (Drake Mungo, 17% of his usual snap share, 3.02 against a 21.93 projection) left in a game they lost by 21.2. Brock Bowers (St. Brown Catholic Church, 19%, 4.8) left in a game decided by 12.12, and the missing 13 points against his projection would have been about all of it. Josh Downs (14.3) and Devin Singletary (0.3) both left early for MOORE, who won anyway.
