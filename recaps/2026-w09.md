# Week 9 Recap: The Cockroach Survives Everything Except Jeantrification

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 9*

## The headline

Look, the Un-killable Cockroach has survived a lot of things in its life, including nuclear winters, Y2K and at least two of my own bad predictions, but it did not survive Jeantrification. We just watched 194.86 go up on the board, the fourth-highest score in league history, for a 100.86-point win in a game the site's model had at 90% for Jeantrification before kickoff. That's the sixth-biggest margin we've ever seen, and for about ten minutes on Sunday it looked like Bijan al-Gaib might make it two of them, until Bijan al-Gaib stopped at 99.64 and the century mark slipped away by 0.36 points.

The star of the show was C.J. Stroud, who went for 35.36 in the superflex against a 15.6 projection, which is the equivalent of ordering the soup and getting a steak dinner. Puka Nacua (35.0), Christian Watson (30.1) and Bucky Irving (27.5) joined him, and the Cockroach's best answer was Kenneth Walker III at 36.3, the top starter score of the week, followed by the other eight starters combining for 57.7, which is like showing up to a gunfight with one guy and eight guys holding binoculars. Kayshon Boutte also dropped 28.5 on the Cockroach bench while Jack Bech put up 1.9 at receiver, and the two were projected a hair apart at 3.87 and 3.81, so go ahead and cry about it but not too hard. Jeantrification is 6-3 and 96% to make the playoffs. The one sour note is that Michael Wilson and Puka Nacua both went to IR, which is a brutal way to follow a 35.0 from Nacua, and the site's odds and bye numbers below still count them, so the cushion is thinner than it looks. The Cockroach is 5-4 and, as of Sunday, no longer un-killable.

## The rest of the slate

**Bijan al-Gaib 182.66, Unsolicited Nix pics 83.02.** This is the other 100-point beatdown, and it deserves its own parade. Jordan Love turned a 16.3 projection into 36.18, Ja'Marr Chase went for 31.5, Bijan Robinson had 25.7, Christian McCaffrey 22.7, and Isaiah Likely chipped in 18.8 from the flex like a guy who was told to bring a salad and showed up with a whole pig. The Nix pics lost Jaylen Waddle almost immediately (19% of his usual snaps, 3.2 points), and Shedeur Sanders then went for 22.68 on the bench behind Caleb Williams' 12.76, which is how you get beat by 99.64. Bijan al-Gaib is 7-2 and we're basically just waiting on the playoffs, while the Nix pics are 5-4 and gave back 13 points of playoff odds.

**St. Brown Catholic Church 150.66, Shough My Hock 117.74.** Faith restored in the Church, which won by 32.92 and is 7-2 after last week's crisis of belief. CeeDee Lamb went for 33.5, Brock Bowers had 25.8 at tight end, Javonte Williams 22.4 and James Cook III 16.7 in the flex, and the Church even got a 5.3 from Jared Goff while Bryce Young scored 20.34 on its bench, which in a closer game would have been a church riot. Shough My Hock got 32.4 from Matthew Golden and 18.8 from KC Concepcion Jr., but it played without Jaylen Warren and Tony Pollard, about 8.6 points off its best lineup, and Jaxon Smith-Njigba could only manage 12.1.

**Hampton Inn 104.90, These Bitches Love 'Doza 54.44.** The site's model gave 'Doza an 8% shot, and 8% is a generous way of describing it. Joe Burrow went for 29.54, Jonah Coleman had 18.8 and Denzel Boston 15.8, and Hampton Inn leaped 12 points of playoff odds to 37%, which is the kind of thing that makes a 4-5 team start looking up the tiebreakers. 'Doza got -2.76 from Carson Wentz at quarterback, which I didn't know was possible without a wager attached, and a 4.1 from Mike Gesicki at tight end while Darren Waller lit up 27.0 on the bench, which is exactly the thing that makes people throw their phones across the room. 'Doza is 1-8 and we're all just doing our best here.

**Captain Jah'Merica 120.30, Tyson chicken tenders 56.42.** A 97% favorite did exactly what a 97% favorite is supposed to, winning by 63.88, which is the fantasy football version of a Nick Saban team playing an FCS opponent. Luther Burden III scored 20.7, Tetairoa McMillan 15.6 and Nico Collins 15.5, even with D'Andre Swift handing back 3.1 against an 18.9 projection. The Tenders got 13.2 from Harold Fannin Jr. and 11.5 from Corey Kiner, then Tank Dell dropped 19.5 on the bench while Jahan Dotson had 3.6 in the superflex, which is the sort of thing that makes you consider a career in accounting. Captain Jah'Merica is 4-5 and the Tenders are 1-8.

**Drake Mungo 107.64, Baby Billys Bible Bonkers 80.60.** The closest game of the week, which is a bit like winning "best dressed" at a funeral. Brock Purdy gave Drake Mungo 25.28 in the superflex and Drake Maye another 18.26, even though Josh Jacobs got hurt and played about 15% of his usual snaps (-0.5). The Bonkers got 18.2 from Justice Hill, who was projected at 6.0, and 16.8 from Chris Olave, then watched Mike Washington Jr. post -0.3 and Tyson Bagent put up a clean 0.0 at quarterback, which we're going to call a statement. Drake Mungo is 6-3 and the Bonkers are 2-7.

## Start/sit report card

- **Costliest bad calls:** Un-killable Cockroach started Jack Bech (projected 3.87, scored 1.9) at receiver over Kayshon Boutte (3.81, scored 28.5), a 26.6-point swing in a game it lost by 100.86, so the Cockroach can't even claim this one was the difference. Baby Billys Bible Bonkers gave away 11.5 starting Mike Washington Jr. (8.15, scored -0.3) over Seth McGowan (6.79, scored 11.2) at running back, and Tyson chicken tenders left 8.6 on the table with Keon Coleman (6.88, scored 4.7) over Quentin Johnston (7.81, scored 13.3). Nobody lost a game on any of these, which is the only silver lining.
- **Bad beats, nobody's fault:** These Bitches Love 'Doza had Mike Gesicki (11.71) at tight end over Darren Waller (7.78), and Waller dropped 27.0 to Gesicki's 4.1, a 22.9-point swing that made zero sense on paper. Tyson chicken tenders started Jahan Dotson (8.6) in the superflex over Tank Dell (2.81), who scored 19.5 to 3.6, and St. Brown Catholic Church saw Jared Goff (18.91, scored 5.3) lose by 15.0 to Bryce Young (16.23, scored 20.34) on its bench. None of them flipped a result, so everybody gets to keep their dignity.
- **Riskiest gut calls:** Baby Billys Bible Bonkers started Justice Hill (projected 6.02) at running back over Seth McGowan (6.79), going against a 0.77-point gap, and Hill went for 18.2 to McGowan's 11.2. Bijan al-Gaib put Isaiah Likely (13.34) in the flex over Tyler Allgeier (13.52) and Likely scored 18.8 to Allgeier's 6.6. Jeantrification started C.J. Stroud (15.6) in the superflex over Daniel Jones (14.43), a 1.2-point gap, and Stroud scored 35.36 to Jones's 10.3, which is the kind of call that makes you want to buy a boat.
- **Dead starts:** none.
- **Left early, nobody's fault:** Josh Jacobs (Drake Mungo, 15% of his usual snap share, -0.5) and Jaylen Waddle (Unsolicited Nix pics, 19%, 3.2) both got hurt early and vanished. Neither changed a result.
