# Week 4 Recap: The Church Breaks the Record Book

*Big Dog Dynasty · 2026 Week 4*

## The headline

St. Brown Catholic Church put up **214.84** points on Bijan al-Gaib, the highest single-week score in league history. The old mark was 195.78 from 2023. Nobody in this league had ever cleared 200 before Sunday.

It was not one guy. CeeDee Lamb went for 41.3, Javonte Williams added 31.3 out of the flex, Zay Flowers chipped in 25.8 and Brock Bowers 23.6. Even the bench was loud, with Brian Robinson sitting on 25.7 points nobody needed. The Church is 4-0, leads the league in points by almost 120, and now carries a 42% title chance in the simulation.

The defending champs never had a shot. Bijan Robinson did his part with 27.7, but Ja'Marr Chase managed 5.7 and Saquon Barkley, acquired in a Week 3 trade, posted a single point in his Bijan al-Gaib debut. Final: 214.84 to 127.66.

## The rest of the slate

**Captain Jah'Merica 156.20, Drake Mungo 150.42.** The game of the week. Tetairoa McMillan had the best day of anyone in the league at 44.2, and Nico Collins added 30.8 to drag Captain Jah'Merica back to .500. Drake Mungo got 25.9 from Chuba Hubbard and 22.8 from Alvin Kamara and still lost by six, with Drake Maye's 24.16 sitting on the bench.

**Jeantrification 164.68, The Hurt Locker 135.80.** Second-highest score of the week. Puka Nacua (27.7) and Tee Higgins (26.7) did the damage, and C.J. Stroud's 23.08 in the superflex spot helped. T.J. Hockenson's 31.4 for The Hurt Locker went to waste; so did Emanuel Wilson's 26.0 on the bench.

**Unsolicited Nix pics 123.76, These Bitches Love 'Doza 78.08.** Kyren Williams went for 36.7 and the Nix pics stay perfect at 4-0. 'Doza is the league's last winless team, and the bench tells the story: Tyquan Thornton 28.1, Michael Mayer 20.2, Darius Cooper 18.3, all unused.

**Hampton Inn 132.24, Homie G Slicer's Team 118.16.** The lineup decision of the week. Homie G started Kenyon Sadiq at tight end and got zero. Colston Loveland sat on the bench with 14.7. The margin was 14.08. That swap alone flips the result. Sam LaPorta's 26.4 was the best tight end line for Hampton Inn, who pulled the upset at 38% odds.

**Big Bucky Hunter 115.52, Baby Billys Bible Bonkers 79.02.** Bucky's first win of the year, and an upset at 28% odds. Kyle Monangai carried it with 28.0. Baby Billys started two players who scored zero, Chris Bell and Greg Dulcich, and finished with the week's second-lowest total.
